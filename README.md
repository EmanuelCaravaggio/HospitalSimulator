OVERVIEW:
The Hospital Simulator is a collabortive project designed to educate and provide hands-on experience 
for individuals interested in pursuing careers in the medical field.The simulator offers a 
variety of interactive scenarios across different various areas of study, helping users explore and 
understand decision-making processes, and patient care methods.

This project was developed in Java as part of a team effort, with each member contributing to the development of distinct 
features and functionalities within the simulator. While the simulator is fully functional, unfortunately, due to the collaborative nature of the project, 
some classes used in the final program may not be present in this repository.
